PT Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma Indonesia, hereinafter referred to as MTID, is a Company engaged in the pharmaceutical industry with a business focus on producing and selling ethical medicines for the Domestic and Overseas markets.

My role as Regulatory Affairs Officer as follows: 
• Prepared and submitted drug variation applications through the Indonesia FDA (BPOM) electronic submission system, ensuring completeness, accuracy, and compliance with current regulatory requirements.
• Arranged and compiled CTD/ACTD dossiers to meet submission requirements, including Administrative, Quality (CMC), Non-Clinical, and Clinical modules, in accordance with BPOM and export country regulations.
• Assessed and classified variation registration categories (major, minor, notification, or other applicable classifications) based on Indonesian regulatory guidelines as well as requirements of export destination countries.
• Conducted regulatory impact assessments to determine documentation needs, approval pathways, and submission strategies for both local and export variations.
• Coordinated and communicated with export country regulatory counterparts and affiliates regarding registration status, variation submission requirements, timelines, and follow-up actions.
• Managed regulatory correspondence related to variation submissions, including responses to authority queries, clarification requests, and post-submission follow-ups.
• Monitored and controlled variation submission timelines to support timely approval and uninterrupted product supply.
Ensured alignment between regulatory submissions, approved dossiers, and product implementation, including labeling and packaging changes where applicable.
• Support export–import regulatory activities for pharmaceutical products across Hong Kong, Myanmar, Vietnam, and India.
