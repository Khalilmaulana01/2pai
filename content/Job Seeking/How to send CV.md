How to mail CV  
  
When someone asked to email your CV,  
You should never send your CV alone without composing a proper email.  
  
Here's an example:  
  
To: [Email address of the company you're applying to]  
Subject: Application for the Position of [Position Title]  
  
Email Body:  
  
Dear [Employer/HR Manager],  
I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to express my interest in the [Position Title] role as advertised. With my skills, qualifications, and experience, I believe I would be a valuable asset to your team.  
  
Attached are my CV and supporting documents for your review. I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how my background aligns with the needs of your organization.  
  
Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to your team and hearing from you soon.  
  
  
Thanks & Regards,  
[Your Full Name]  
[Your Contact Number]  
[Your Email Address]  
  
Important Notes:  
Attach your CV in PDF format to maintain professionalism. Avoid sending it as a scanned document, image, or screenshot.  
  
Carefully re-read your email to avoid any type of errors.
